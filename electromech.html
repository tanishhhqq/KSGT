ABOUT THE COMPANY

We believe in the philosophy that "Action with Vision can make a positive difference". Our strength is our technical expertise with high technology products and its use in providing solutions to our customers. Our team works closely with the customer in all phases of the process. KRISH GROUP has considerable depth and width of experience in instruments, controls, and systems. We act as a bridge between technology and application. We are in a position to provide these technologies and products depending on your requirement. We have established ourselves, as a leading and reputed firm dealing with prestigious organizations such as Navy, ONGC, etc. We have made a mark in different sectors like Process Control Execution, BMS, Automobile, Food Industry and Pharmaceutical.


We Execute Projects in Industrial Automation , Instrumentation Erection & Commisioning , Fabrication & Erection , BMS , AMC & PPM
