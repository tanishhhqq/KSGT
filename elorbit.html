About Us

Elorbit Industries is a leading manufacturer and exporter of high-quality cable trays and accessories, specializing in ladder-type, perforated, and wire mesh trays. Based in Pune, India, we serve diverse industrial sectors with precision-engineered solutions for cable management. With a commitment to quality, customization, and durability, Elorbit offers products in various materials and finishes including hot-dip galvanized, powder-coated, and stainless steel. Our focus on innovation, compliance with global standards, and customer satisfaction makes us a trusted partner in infrastructure and industrial projects across India and abroad.
